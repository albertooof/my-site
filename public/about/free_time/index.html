Hobbies

Outside of my studies and work, judo has been a significant part of my life for the past 20 years. I represented Great Britain in judo at the European Universities Games in 2024 and 2025 (British Judo Association article), and in 2025 I was awarded a Full Blue for Sporting Excellence by the University of Glasgow (link). I also coach both children and adults, which has given me experience working with people at different levels and ages.

More recently, I have been working towards my Cytech bike mechanic qualification. I enjoy restoring and repairing 80s and 90s mountain bikes, and once I have gained more experience, I would like to become more involved with community bike shops.
