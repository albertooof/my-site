Outside of my studies and work, **judo has been a significant part of my life for the past 20 years**. I represented *Great Britain* in judo at the **European Universities Games in 2024 and 2025** ([British Judo Association article](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vvP2k5JaTgAxS-8oopCLXvOsfrvXc6Y0/view?usp=sharing)), and in 2025 I was awarded a **Full Blue for Sporting Excellence by the University of Glasgow** ([link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mSdoP0vmZxtjemFB-ce6tNHO84YeHVq3/view?usp=drive_link)). I also coach both *children and adults*, which has given me experience working with people at different levels and ages.

More recently, I have been working towards my **Cytech bike mechanic qualification**. I enjoy restoring and repairing **80s and 90s mountain bikes**, and once I have gained more experience, I would like to become more involved with *community bike shops*.
