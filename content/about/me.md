I’m a **PhD graduate in Cognitive Neuroscience** with experience in *data analysis, human-subject research, and clinical research* across academic, hospital, and industry settings in Europe, the US, and Japan.

My work has involved **study design, participant coordination, statistical modelling, multimodal data analysis, and reporting tools** using R and Python. I’ve also worked with **neuroimaging, neurostimulation, behavioural research, and regulatory-compliant data** in ethics-approved studies.

I’m comfortable moving between **analysis, research coordination, and communication**, particularly when working with people from different technical backgrounds. My experience covers both the research and operational sides of studies, from working with participants and managing research data to analysing results and communicating findings. I’m interested in opportunities at the intersection of *healthcare, research, data, and technology*.

Outside of research, I **coach Judo** and **repair bikes** :))
